FAQ

Shipping, warranties, payment options, and store hours.

What shipping options do you offer and how fast are they?

  • Standard Shipping: delivered within 3–5 business days. Free on orders over $50.
  • Express Shipping: delivered within 1–2 business days for an additional flat rate calculated at checkout.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, NovaMart ships across the domestic region only. We do not support international shipping at this time.

What is covered under the product warranty?

  • All electronic products sold by NovaMart come with a standard 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty.
  • The warranty covers manufacturing defects, hardware faults, and malfunctioning components under normal usage. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid exposure, unauthorized repairs, or general wear and tear.

What payment methods are accepted online and in-store?

  • Major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover)
  • Mobile digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
  • NovaMart digital gift cards

What are NovaMart's store operating hours?

  • Monday – Friday: 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM
  • Saturday: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM

How can I reach Customer Support?

  • Email: support@novamart.com
  • Phone: 1-800-555-NOVA (available during normal store hours)

Return policy

NovaMart aims for complete satisfaction with every purchase. These are the return rules.

1. Return window

Customers may return eligible products within 30 days of the original purchase or delivery date.

2. Proof of purchase

A valid receipt, tax invoice, or online order confirmation is required for all returns, exchanges, or store credit.

3. General conditions

  • Items must be unused, unwashed, and in the same condition as received.
  • Items must be in their original packaging, complete with all included accessories, tags, manuals, and promotional materials.

4. Exclusions

  • Opened electronics: smart home devices, audio equipment, or personal electronics that have been opened, unsealed, or powered on are non-returnable unless verified as defective upon arrival under warranty terms.
  • Perishable items: opened food products, fresh produce, and refrigerated items cannot be returned.
  • Clearance items: products marked Final Sale or bought during clearance events are non-refundable.

5. Refunds

  • Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the item is received and inspected.
  • Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a NovaMart mistake or a damaged product.
